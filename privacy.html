1.Who we are

thyagulabs (“thyagulabs”, “we”, “us”) designs and builds mobile apps, web apps and digital products. The company responsible for your information is thyagulabs, #28/4, Whitefield – Kundalahalli main road, Siddapura, 4th floor, Above Prima Diagnostics, Bengaluru – 66, India.

For any privacy question or request, email support@thyagulabs.com.

2.What this policy covers

This policy covers information collected through this website, including the discovery-call form. It does not cover information we process while delivering a project for a client. That is governed by the written agreement we sign with that client.

3.Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit the discovery-call form, we receive:

  • your name and job title
  • your email address
  • your contact number, if you choose to give it
  • the region and services you select
  • anything you write about your project, such as scope or start date

If you email or call us, we receive whatever you include in that message.

Information collected automatically

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not set any cookies of our own. To show you the website, our hosting provider and font provider receive standard technical information from your browser, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. See section 6.

4.How we use it

We use the information you give us to:

  • reply to your enquiry and arrange a discovery call with the right regional team
  • understand your project and prepare a proposal or quote
  • follow up about that enquiry
  • keep records of our conversations, and meet our legal and accounting obligations

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists unless you ask us to.

5.Legal bases

Where laws such as the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation apply, we rely on:

  • Steps before a contract: when you ask us about a project, we use your details to respond and prepare a proposal.
  • Legitimate interests: running and improving our business, and keeping records of enquiries.
  • Legal obligation: when we must keep or share information by law.
  • Consent: where the law requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Where India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies, by submitting the form you consent to us processing the information you provide for the purposes in section 4. You may withdraw that consent by emailing us.

7.International transfers

We work from offices in Germany, the United States, Canada, India, Singapore and the United Arab Emirates, and our service providers operate globally. Your information may therefore be processed outside the country where you live. When we transfer it, we take steps to protect it as required by applicable law, for example by using standard contractual clauses where needed.

8.How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client. Client records are then kept for as long as our agreement and the law require. You can ask us to delete your enquiry earlier at any time.

9.Security

This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we limit access to enquiry details to the people who need them. No system is completely secure, so please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information through the form.

10.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or update it
  • ask us to delete it
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it
  • withdraw consent you have given
  • receive your information in a portable format

To use any of these rights, email support@thyagulabs.com. We will reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

These rights come from laws including the EU and UK GDPR, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, Canada’s PIPEDA, Singapore’s PDPA, the UAE’s Personal Data Protection Law and US state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

11.Children

This website is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make significant changes, we will make that clear on this page.
